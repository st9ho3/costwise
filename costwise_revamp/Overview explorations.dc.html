Turn 2 · Greek type

Three Greek-capable type systems

I checked the Google Fonts charsets directly rather than trusting my memory: Bricolage Grotesque, Nunito Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Baloo 2 all ship without Greek — Latin, Latin-ext, Cyrillic and Vietnamese only. So the Greek screen in 1a has no brand type in it at all. Each option below sets the same headline, sentence and money row in faces that do cover Greek, with the Latin equivalent underneath so you can see what the swap costs on the English side.

2a The sharp grotesque

Geologica for display, Fira Sans for everything spoken, Fira Mono for money. Geologica is the nearest thing to Bricolage's optical sharpness that has Greek; Fira's Greek was drawn properly and covers greek-ext.

Where I'd land

2a. It reads closest to what you have, it's one decision instead of two type systems, and Fira's Greek is the best-drawn of the three. 2c is the safe answer if English is the primary market and you don't want to re-approve the Latin look. Either way the wordmark is a separate problem — Baloo 2 has no Greek, and "Costwise" stays Latin anyway.
